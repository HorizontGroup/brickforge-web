## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a warm, premium, tactile universe designed for modular brick building and creative assembly.

### Visual Character
- **Modular Physicality:** UI elements look like precision-molded ABS components
- **Physical Elevation:** Crisp offset shadows mimic studio desktop lighting
- **Warm Editorial Canvas:** Porcelain and sun-washed ivory surfaces
- **Graphic Directness:** Solid color blocking, intentional white space, bold geometric silhouettes
